## Goal

Serve the Products & upsell tab, product detail, payment method share, coupons, currencies and the order value histogram **as on-demand query views** (`view: "products"` / `"distributions"` on `POST …/queries`). No HTTP routes here: the modeler (P4) runs these runners from its queue.

## Context

Part of PRD-006. **Parts 5–6 win.** Implement the `ViewRunner` interface from `src/analytics/views.ts` (5.18) and replace the P0 stub runners in `products/view.ts` and `distributions/view.ts`.

## Acceptance criteria

- **products** params: `from`, `to`, `report_currency`, `product_id?`, `purchase_type: all | initial | upsell`, `search?`, `sort`, `limit` (default 8), `offset`.
  - Products × variants tree: per variation `units_added`, `units_purchased`, `units_share`, `buy_to_cart`, `revenue`, `revenue_share`; `variants[]` with `units`, `share_of_product`, `revenue_share`, `low_sample` (control units < 15); beyond the limit one "Other products" row with no variants; top 10 variants + Other.
  - Units from `event_items` with `has(exp_ids, E)`, attributed via `buildAttributedEvents` to the exposure variation; revenue at the previous day's FX rate; buy_to_cart per PRD §2.3 (min per visitor × product).
- **distributions** params: `distributions[]` of `payment_type` (basis `add_payment_info` events), `coupon` (top 10 + "Other codes (n)" + "No coupon"), `currency`, `order_value` (custom bins or 8 pooled quantile bins; respects `cap_outliers`).
- No stats in the result (crobot calls `POST /api/v1/stats` if it needs them).
- `validate()` throws the PRD §5.5 400 codes.

## Tests

- F1 products and distributions results equal `docs/reference/samples/f1/modeled/{products,distributions}.json`.
- Shares sum to 1 per variation.
