## Goal

Revenue in every daily row is converted per order into the reporting currency at the **previous UTC day's** rate (the GA4 model). The FX sync exists (Frankfurter / ECB daily, USD base, cross rates); this task makes the lookup reliable.

## Acceptance criteria

- `fxSqlExpr(currencyCol, tsCol, reportCurrency)`: a ClickHouse expression the modeler and views use, converting at `toDate(ts) - 1`, falling back to the **last known rate on or before** that day.
- `backfill(fromDate, toDate)`: fetches and inserts any missing days (ECB skips weekends and holidays → carry the previous business day forward); idempotent. Run on collector start for the last 400 days and after each daily sync.
- Same-currency orders skip lookup (rate 1). Unknown currency → the order is excluded from `sum` and counted in `quality.unconvertible_orders`.
- Reporting currency comes from the subscription / query (`report_currency`), set per project by crobot.

## Tests

- Weekend order uses Friday's rate; missing day falls back; idempotent backfill; unknown currency excluded and counted.
