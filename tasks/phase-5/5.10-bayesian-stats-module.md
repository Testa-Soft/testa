## Goal

Pure, deterministic stats: posteriors → draws → chance to beat / best, lift, CI, expected loss.

## Context

Part of PRD-006 (`docs/prds/006-ecommerce-events-and-metric-modeling.md`). **Part 5 of the PRD wins over earlier sections when they disagree.** Defaults in §5.1 are decided; don't re-ask.

## Acceptance criteria

- `xoshiro128**` seeded from xxhash32 of the §5.5 key; Gamma (Marsaglia–Tsang), Beta, Normal (Box–Muller), Student-t.
- Models: Beta–Binomial (flat prior) and Normal on the mean (Student-t posterior) for AOV (capped values), RPV and engagement. No log-normal (decided).
- `summarize()` returns `chance_to_beat_control`, `chance_to_be_best`, `lift`, `lift_ci95`, `expected_loss`; control gets `chance_to_be_best` only.
- `insufficient_data` thresholds per §5.1.
- No I/O; Float64Array draws.
- Part 6 additions: Normal model on per-order / per-unit observations (basket_size, avg_item_price), `confidence` parameter for `lift_ci`, `direction: 'lower'` support (chance_to_beat flips), and pure helpers `sampleSizeTwoProportion({baseline, mde, alpha, power})` and `srmChiSquare(observed, weights)`.

## Implementation notes

- Pure module: no ClickHouse imports, so it's trivially unit-testable.

## Tests

- Golden test: 115/4,737 vs 137/4,790 (+109/4,702) → chance to beat 0.907±0.005, chance to be best 0.876±0.005.
- Determinism: same input gives byte-identical output.
- Parameter recovery for the Normal mean model on simulated skewed order values (≥ 200 orders).
- Planning golden: baseline 0.024277, mde 0.15, α 0.05, power 0.8 → 30,084 ±1. SRM golden: [4737, 4790, 4702], ⅓ each → χ² 0.8277, p 0.6611 (±0.001).
- Performance: 3 variations × 15 metrics × 100k draws in under 500 ms.
