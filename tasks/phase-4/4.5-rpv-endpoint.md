## Goal

Serve `GET /api/v1/metrics/rpv` → `RpvSummary`: per-variation revenue-per-visitor
(converted revenue ÷ unique exposed visitors) with a **bootstrap** CI and
bootstrap significance vs the reference variation.

## Acceptance criteria

- Valid `RpvSummary`: per variation `rpv = total_revenue / visitors`,
  `total_revenue`, `visitors`, `sample_size = visitors`, `ci_low`/`ci_high` from
  `bootstrapRpv` (4.2).
- `visitors` = `uniqExactMerge` of `experiment_view` visitors (4.3), NOT order
  count — RPV's denominator is everyone exposed, including non-purchasers.
- `significance[]` via `bootstrapSignificance` over per-visitor revenue arrays
  (4.3 `perVisitorRevenue`, zero-filled for exposed non-purchasers).
- Deterministic bootstrap seed (per request, e.g. derived from
  `experiment_id`+window) so repeated calls are stable — never `Math.random()`.
- `visitors = 0` → `rpv = 0`, null/zero CI, excluded from significance.

## Implementation notes

- The per-visitor sample MUST include zeros for exposed visitors who never
  purchased, or RPV and its CI are both overstated. Build it in 4.3 by
  left-joining exposed visitors to their summed converted revenue (0 default).
- Reference-variation selection mirrors 4.4 (control heuristic + optional
  `reference_variation_id` override) — factor the shared selection helper.

## Tests

- Seeded CH: exposed visitors with mixed purchase behaviour → assert `rpv`,
  stable CI under a fixed seed, and significance direction.
- Zero-inflation: many exposed non-purchasers pull RPV down and widen the CI vs
  an orders-only computation (guards against the denominator bug).
- `visitors = 0` → zeros, not NaN.

## Out of scope

- AOV / sessions / funnel.
- Variance-reduction (CUPED) — note as a future enhancement.
